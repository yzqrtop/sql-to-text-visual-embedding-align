9 group_by_having
Q: Show departments with more than 10 students.
SELECT dept, COUNT(*) FROM students GROUP BY dept HAVING COUNT(*) > 10
结构: GROUP BY + HAVING — Stack + Funnel after aggregation (Scope边界)

Q: Average score and total students per class.

11 inner_join
Q: List students and their enrolled courses.

12 left_join
Q: All departments and their employees (include empty depts).
SELECT d.name, e.name FROM departments d LEFT JOIN employees e ON d.id = e.dept_id
结构: LEFT JOIN — Asymmetric branch (保留左容器所有行)
13 join_where
Q: Engineering employees earning over 10000.
SELECT e.name, d.name FROM employees e JOIN departments d ON e.dept_id = d.id WHERE d.name='Engineering' AND e.salary > 10000
结构: JOIN + WHERE — Two containers + Branch + Funnel after
14 triple_join
Q: Students, their courses, and professors.
SELECT s.name, c.title, p.name FROM students s JOIN enrollments e ON s.id = e.sid JOIN courses c ON e.cid = c.id JOIN professors p ON c.pid = p.id
结构: 3-way JOIN — Multiple connected containers (高join_density)
15 order_limit
Q: Top 5 highest paid employees.

结构: ORDER BY + LIMIT — Sorted result + capped output
16 subquery_where_in
Q: Students enrolled in Computer Science courses.

17 subquery_comparison
Q: Employees earning above average.

18 correlated_subquery
Q: Employees earning more than their dept average.
SELECT e.name FROM employees e WHERE e.salary > (SELECT AVG(salary) FROM employees WHERE dept_id = e.dept_id)
结构: Correlated subquery — Outer-Inner scope with linkage + Boundary
19 derived_table_from
Q: Top earners from the aggregated result.
SELECT dept, avg_sal FROM (SELECT dept, AVG(salary) AS avg_sal FROM employees GROUP BY dept) t WHERE avg_sal > 8000
结构: Subquery in FROM — Boundary wraps aggregated inner result
20 nested_nested_subquery
Q: Students in courses taught by professors who have >5 students.
SELECT s.name FROM students s WHERE s.id IN (SELECT sid FROM enrollments WHERE cid IN (SELECT id FROM courses WHERE pid IN (SELECT pid FROM professors GROUP BY pid HAVING COUNT(*) > 5)))
结构: 3-level Nested — Multiple nested boundaries (高nest_depth)
21 full_join_group_having
Q: Depts with avg salary > 7000 for employees > 30 years old.
SELECT d.name, AVG(e.salary) FROM departments d JOIN employees e ON d.id = e.dept_id WHERE e.age > 30 GROUP BY d.name HAVING AVG(e.salary) > 7000
结构: FULL PIPELINE — JOIN + WHERE + GROUP BY + HAVING (所有原语组合)
22 window_function
Q: Ranked salaries within each department.
SELECT name, dept, RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY dept ORDER BY salary DESC) AS rnk FROM employees
结构: Window Function — Partition-aware stack + ordered result
23 union_set_operation
Q: All customers and suppliers from New York.
SELECT name, 'Customer' FROM customers WHERE city='NY' UNION SELECT name, 'Supplier' FROM suppliers WHERE city='NY'
结构: UNION — Two stacked result sets with set operation
24 complex_composite
Q: Most popular course per major for CS students over 20 with 3+ courses.
SELECT m.major, c.title, COUNT(*) FROM majors m JOIN students s ON m.id = s.mid JOIN enrollments e ON s.id = e.sid JOIN courses c ON e.cid = c.id WHERE m.name='CS' AND s.age > 20 GROUP BY m.major, c.title HAVING COUNT(*) >= 3 ORDER BY COUNT(*) DESC
结构: COMPOSITE — 4-table JOIN + WHERE + GROUP BY + HAVING + ORDER BY
